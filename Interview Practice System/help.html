✦ Quick Fixes

Common Troubleshooting

Solutions for frequently encountered platform questions.

⏱️

Timer reached zero before submitting

Don't worry! When the 20-minute countdown expires, all answered questions are automatically submitted and calculated into your final score.

📄

Resume upload failed or format error

Ensure your file is in `.pdf` format and under 5MB. Multi-column graphical templates may lower ATS parsing accuracy; standard single-column text PDFs work best.

🔄

Test score not showing in dashboard

Check if your browser is in private/incognito mode or if cookies/localStorage are disabled. Interfinix stores test progress in local storage for quick access.

🔑

How to change password or user name

Log in, click on the Hamburger menu (☰) in `main.html`, and select "My Profile" (`myprofile.html`) to edit your contact and academic information.
