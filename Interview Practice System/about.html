✦ How We Started

The Story Behind Interfinix

Interfinix started as a college project — a group of final-year students who noticed a massive gap between what universities taught and what companies actually asked in interviews.

"We spent our last semester building the platform we wish we had in our first year."

What started as a simple question bank grew into a full-fledged platform with 9 domains, an analytics dashboard, resume-based personalisation, and a mentor query system. Today, Interfinix helps thousands of students across India crack interviews at top companies.

Our platform is built by students, for students — with expert guidance, real interview patterns, and a laser focus on helping you land the job you deserve.
