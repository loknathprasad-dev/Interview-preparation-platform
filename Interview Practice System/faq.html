🌐 General
💡
What is Interfinix?
▼
Interfinix is a structured interview preparation platform built for students and job seekers. It offers domain-specific MCQ test banks, personalised study roadmaps, a resume-based question generator, a performance dashboard, and a mentor query support system — all in one place.
Platform Overview
💸
Is Interfinix free to use?
▼
Yes! Interfinix offers a completely free plan that gives you access to all 9 domains, the test engine, study roadmaps, and your performance dashboard. There are no hidden fees or credit card requirements to sign up.
Pricing
🎓
Who is Interfinix designed for?
▼
Interfinix is built primarily for:

• College students preparing for campus placements
• Fresh graduates seeking their first technical role
• Working professionals looking to switch domains
• Hackathon teams brushing up technical fundamentals
Audience
🗂️
How many job domains does the platform cover?
▼
Interfinix currently covers 9 in-demand job domains: Web Development, Java Developer, Cloud Computing, Frontend Developer, Backend Developer, Full Stack Developer, Machine Learning Engineer, Cyber Security, and DSA. More domains are planned based on student demand.
Domains
📱
Can I use Interfinix on my phone?
▼
Absolutely. Interfinix is fully responsive and works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers. You can take tests, view your dashboard, and access roadmaps from any device without needing to install an app.
Accessibility
👤 Account
✍️
How do I create an account?
▼
Click Sign Up from the homepage or navbar. Fill in your name, email, and password — the whole process takes under 30 seconds. No credit card or email verification required.
Registration
🔑
I forgot my password. What do I do?
▼
On the Login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't appear in your inbox.
Login
🪪
How do I update my profile information?
▼
After logging in, go to My Profile from the hamburger menu in the top navigation. Click "Edit Profile" to update your name, target domains, skills, certifications, and experience.
Profile
🗑️
How do I delete my account?
▼
To delete your account, go to My Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your test history and data will be permanently removed.
Account Management
🚪
How do I log out of my account?
▼
Open the hamburger menu (☰) in the top navigation bar and click Logout. Your session will be cleared securely. You can also close the browser window — your session will remain active for 7 days for convenience.
Session
📝 Tests & Practice
📝
How does the test engine work?
▼
Each test presents a set of domain-specific MCQs. You select one answer per question. A live countdown timer keeps you on pace. After submission, you'll receive a detailed score breakdown with correct answers and explanations for every question.
Test Format
⏱️
How long is each test? Is there a timer?
▼
Each test contains 20 questions with a default timer of 20 minutes (1 minute per question). The timer is visible throughout the test and will auto-submit when it reaches zero. You can also submit early.
Timer
⏸️
Can I pause a test and come back to it?
▼
No — once a test begins, the timer runs continuously to simulate real interview conditions. Refreshing or navigating away will auto-submit your current answers. We recommend completing the test in one uninterrupted session.
Test Rules
🔄
Can I retake a test?
▼
Yes! You can retake any domain test as many times as you want. Each attempt is logged separately in your Dashboard, so you can track how your scores improve over time. Questions may vary between attempts.
Retakes
📊
How is my score calculated and shown?
▼
Each correct answer earns 1 mark. There is no negative marking. After submission, you'll see your total score, percentage, time taken, a per-question breakdown (correct / wrong), and an explanation for every answer.
Scoring
🚩
Can I flag questions for review during the test?
▼
Yes. During the test, click the 🚩 Flag icon next to any question to mark it for review. A question panel on the right side shows which questions are answered, unanswered, or flagged so you can easily navigate between them.
Test Navigation
🗃️
How many questions are available per domain?
▼
Each domain has a bank of 25+ carefully curated questions, with more added regularly. Tests draw questions randomly from the bank so each attempt feels fresh. Our faculty mentors review and update the question bank every month.
Question Bank

📄 Resume Upload
📤
How does the Resume Upload feature work?
▼
Go to Upload Resume, drag and drop your PDF resume, and select your target domain. The platform analyses your resume content and generates:

• An ATS compatibility score
• Keyword match analysis against the selected domain
• Personalised interview questions based on your experience
Resume Analysis
📎
What file formats are supported for resume upload?
▼
Currently, we support PDF format only (up to 5MB). We recommend uploading a clean, single-column PDF for the best ATS analysis results. DOCX support is coming soon.
File Types
🔒
Is my resume stored securely?
▼
Your resume is processed locally in the browser session and is not permanently stored on our servers. We do not share your resume data with any third party. You can delete your uploaded resume at any time from the Upload Resume page.
Privacy
🎯
What does the ATS score mean?
▼
The ATS (Applicant Tracking System) score indicates how well your resume is likely to pass automated screening filters used by companies. A score of:

• 80–100 → Excellent — likely to pass most ATS filters
• 60–79 → Good — minor improvements recommended
• Below 60 → Needs work — follow the improvement tips provided
ATS Analysis
💬 Query & Support
🎟️
How do I raise a query or ask for help?
▼
Visit Query Support from the main dashboard menu. Fill in the subject, domain, and your question. A support ticket is created instantly and you'll receive a response from our faculty mentors within 24 hours.
Query System
📋
How do I check the status of my query?
▼
All your raised queries are visible on the Query Support page under "My Tickets". Each ticket shows its status: Open, In Review, or Resolved, along with the mentor's response once available.
Ticket Tracking
⏳
How quickly will I get a response to my query?
▼
Most queries are answered within 24 hours on weekdays. Complex technical questions may take up to 48 hours. Urgent queries can be marked as High Priority when submitting, which notifies mentors immediately.
Response Time
📣
How do I report a bug or give feedback?
▼
Use the Feedback page (accessible from the hamburger menu after login). You can rate the platform, describe issues, and suggest improvements. For critical bugs, mark your submission as "Bug Report" so it reaches our development team with highest priority.
Feedback
